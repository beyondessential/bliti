Test an Iti

Follow this guide to test an assembled Iti and mark it tested.

Testing

  1. Plug in the network cable and the power. The Iti boots by itself.
    photo or video
  2. Connect to the Iti with the operator app over Bluetooth.
    photo or video
  3. Set up Tailscale from the app, if provisioning did not.
    photo or video
  4. Check the Case tile in the app's device view reads closed. That confirms the lid presses the CASE switch.
    photo or video
  5. Press the USER button through the closed case, with a thin tool such as the back of a pair of tweezers.
    photo or video
  6. Run the test procedure. to be written
    photo or video
  7. Mark the Iti as tested in inventory.
    photo or video
