Tamanu Iti network setup

Three changes to the networking flow. One instructional three-way choice, cable or join a wifi network or share the Iti's own wifi, presented as one-at-a-time even though the Iti can do more at once under Advanced. Sharing drawn like a phone hotspot. And no separate save: a change takes effect and is kept, with an Undo offered for a short window instead of a confirm step. Lanes A and B are two homes for the choice, to compare.

A. One "Network" entry on the Status screen, opening a chooser

The two task rows (Wifi, Share wifi) become one "Network" row that shows the mode in force. Tapping it opens a chooser: a short decision screen that switches the Iti to the mode picked, then shows that mode's detail. Reads as a setup step.

Status. One Network row, showing the mode in force.
