Measurement and fact catalogue

Every reading the device reports today, restated as a measurement or a fact with its traits. Green is a fact (no state, no history); the rest are readings. The last column says what it was before, so nothing is silently dropped.

Units are spelled out. kind names what the value is, and its vocabulary is open. Where the device holds a piece of information, it sends it rather than leaving it implied.

What a trait is

A trait is a dimension you can aggregate across. It slices one measurement into instances, and re-combining those instances — summing, ranking, comparing — means something. That is the whole test, and it is what separates a trait from two measurements that merely look alike.

traitslicesre-combining gives
interfacethroughput per linktotal traffic, by summing
directionthroughput per way it runstotal traffic, by summing
filesystemusage per mountthe fullest, by ranking
sensortemperature per probethe hottest, by ranking

Most traits also name a subject you could point at — a link, a mount, a probe. direction does not, which is why naming a subject is a useful habit rather than the test.

Two rejected traits, by the same test. part: used | total re-combines to nothing: a fraction and a byte count do not sum or rank. condition: undervoltage | speed-capped re-combines to nothing either: they are two unrelated questions, not one question about two subjects. Both are two measurements wearing one name, and both take two names instead.

Identity

typenamekindtraitswas
facthostnametext—hostname
factboardtext—board headline
factboard-revisiontext—board detail "Revision"
factostext—os headline
factkerneltext—os detail "Kernel"
factlast-bootdatetime—uptime

Uptime becomes the instant it booted. An uptime changes every second and is a subtraction away from a boot time that does not change at all. The wire carries the instant; the client says "5d 14h".

This one needs a clock. at is boot-relative precisely because a device in the field may have no set clock and no way to reach one. A device that cannot answer for its boot instant omits last-boot, as it omits any reading its hardware and OS cannot answer for — which also makes the missing clock visible, where an uptime quietly hid it.

The network group collision dissolves. Addresses and throughput were both grouped as network, which is what forced the per-interface readings to be summed away. They are now separate names, and per-interface throughput is a first-class reading with its own state, error and history.

Compute

typenamekind · unittraitswas
readingcpu-usagefraction—cpu
readingcpu-frequencyquantity · hertz—throttling detail "Speed"
factcpu-frequency-maxquantity · hertz—throttling detail "Full speed"
readingundervoltageboolean—throttling text summary
readingfrequency-cappedboolean—throttling text summary
readingmemory-usagefraction—memory
factmemory-totalquantity · bytes—memory detail "Used" and "Total"
Throttling becomes two measurements, not a sentence. The device currently composes "Undervolted and slowed" as text, which a client cannot act on. Two named conditions, each with its own state, is the same information as data, and this is the one place a new boolean kind is wanted.
frequency-capped is not cpu-frequency < cpu-frequency-max. Ordinary idle scaling lowers the frequency too, so the comparison would report throttling on a device that is simply not busy. The platform's own flag is a distinct fact and is not derivable from the two frequencies.

The boot-partition rule moves to the client, and needs a trait to move on. The device currently excludes boot partitions from the headline itself. It now states the role and the client applies the policy — which is the same shape as the address headline moving client-side.

The device's "one mount per block device, shortest path wins" rule stays on the device: it is deduplication of what to report, not a judgement about what matters.

What this catalogue decides

Three device-side judgements become client rules. Which address to headline, which filesystem sets the storage headline, and which temperature sensor leads. In each case the device gains a trait stating the fact — route, role, sensor — and stops choosing.
Two traits were rejected by the aggregation test while drafting this: part and condition. Both were two measurements wearing one name. part gave it away by reading as used against memory and free against filesystems, the same name for opposite quantities.
A fraction and a total, and every other figure comes from those two. Used bytes, free bytes and percentages are all arithmetic on a fraction and a total, so only those two cross the wire. The division also falls where the data does: the fraction is what moves and is compact, the total is a fact that does not move.
Details that were the same number twice collapse: battery charge appeared as both the fraction headline and a percentage in its detail.
One new kind, boolean, so throttling conditions are data rather than an English sentence. Everything else uses text, quantity, fraction, duration, ipv4 and ipv6.
