Where the nesting goes

The same device, the same moment, rendered three ways. What differs is only how much of a reading's insides are readings in their own right. In every column the client picks the order and the labels, renders what it knows bespoke, and appends what it does not as a plain card.

Network is expanded in all three because it is where nesting is settled. Disk and Memory are expanded because they are where the three options actually part company.

1 · detail becomes readings

One mechanism. Every sub-value is a reading with a name, a state and a history. Disk's filesystems, memory's totals and each address all gain their own scale, colour and graph.

2 · readings alongside detail

Two mechanisms. A sub-fact that stands on its own is a child reading; a figure that only qualifies its parent stays in detail. A filesystem is its own fact, memory's totals are not.
